Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

1. Summary

Skylark is an offline-first music player. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

2. Data stored on your device

All of your data stays on your device only: your library, playlists, favorites, play history, downloaded audio files, imported files, lyrics cache, cover-art cache, equalizer settings, and app preferences. Deleting the app removes all of this data permanently.

3. Network requests

When you use online features, the app makes direct requests from your device to third-party servers, limited to:

  • Music streaming and search: content and metadata are retrieved from the public interfaces of third-party content platforms when you search or play online music.
  • Lyrics: synchronized lyrics are fetched from lrclib.net, a public lyrics service, queried by track title and artist.
  • Cover art: album artwork images are loaded from the content platform's public image CDN.
  • Playlist import: when you paste a playlist link from a music service, the app fetches that playlist's public metadata from the service, then matches tracks by title.

These requests contain only the content needed to serve your request (e.g. a search term or a track identifier). They are not used by us to build profiles. What those third parties do with requests to their own servers is governed by their own policies.

4. Local network

The Wi-Fi import feature runs a temporary HTTP server on your local network so a computer on the same network can upload audio files to the app. Files received are stored locally and are never sent anywhere else. The Share extension receives files you explicitly share into the app and stores them locally.

5. Permissions

The app requests access to your photo library only when you choose to import a video (to extract its audio) or pick an image (as wallpaper). It requests access to music files metadata only for files you import. No permission is used in the background for collection purposes.

6. Children

Skylark is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you anywhere, there is nothing to export, correct, or delete on our side. To remove all local data, uninstall the app or use the in-app clear-history / clear-cache options.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-25

1. 摘要

Skylark 是一款离线优先的音乐播放器。我们不收集、不上传、不出售、不共享你的任何个人数据。没有账号体系，没有统计分析，没有广告，没有任何形式的追踪。

2. 存储在你设备上的数据

你的全部数据仅保存在你的设备本地：曲库、歌单、收藏、播放历史、下载的音频、导入的文件、歌词缓存、封面缓存、均衡器设置与应用偏好。卸载应用即永久删除全部数据。

3. 网络请求

使用在线功能时，应用会从你的设备直接向第三方服务器发起请求，仅限于：

  • 音乐播放与搜索：搜索或播放在线音乐时，从第三方内容平台的公开接口获取内容与元数据。
  • 歌词：按歌曲名与歌手向公开歌词服务 lrclib.net 拉取同步歌词。
  • 封面图：从内容平台的公开图片 CDN 加载专辑封面。
  • 歌单导入：粘贴音乐平台的歌单链接时，向该平台拉取歌单公开元数据，并按标题逐曲匹配。

这些请求只包含完成功能所必需的内容（如搜索词或曲目标识）。我们不用它们建立用户画像。第三方如何处理其服务器收到的请求，由其自身政策决定。

4. 局域网

Wi-Fi 导入功能会在局域网内启动临时 HTTP 服务，便于同一网络下的电脑向应用上传音频文件。接收的文件仅存本地，不会发送到任何其他地方。分享扩展只接收你主动分享进来的文件并本地保存。

5. 权限

仅当你选择导入视频（抽取音频）或选取图片（作壁纸）时，应用才会请求相册访问；仅对你导入的音乐文件请求媒体元数据访问。任何权限都不用于后台收集。

6. 儿童隐私

Skylark 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的数据。

7. 你的权利

因为我们没有持有你的任何数据，所以无需提供导出、更正或删除服务。卸载应用或使用应用内的清空历史 / 清缓存功能即可移除全部本地数据。

8. 政策变更

如有变更，将在本页面发布更新版本并标注新日期。
